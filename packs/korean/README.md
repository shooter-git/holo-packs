# Korean

The gayageum, the Korean zither with twelve silk strings, plucked with the fingers. Its sound is warm
and round, and its soul is in the left hand, which presses and shakes the strings after each pluck
(nonghyeon): **Gayageum, Deep Vibrato** leans into that.

**Sources:** modelled by HO[LO] Desk's synths; there's no free gayageum recording that may be shipped
yet.
